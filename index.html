About

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering student focused on full-custom and semi-custom IC layout in Cadence Virtuoso on the GPDK 45nm process. My work spans schematic design, layout implementation, and physical verification — with particular attention to device matching, symmetry, and parasitic-aware placement and routing.

I'm comfortable across the stack: from analog building blocks like current mirrors, differential pairs, and OTAs, to digital logic, low-power techniques such as MTCMOS and power gating, and sign-off checks including DRC, LVS, and reliability analysis for IR drop, electromigration, latch-up, and antenna effects.
